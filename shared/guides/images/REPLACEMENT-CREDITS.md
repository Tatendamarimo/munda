# Maize guide photo credits

All 22 guide entries now use real reference photographs. The seven replacements below replace the former drawings and the low-resolution ear-rot photo. Images remain bundled for offline use. License verification is not an agronomic review; `confirmed` remains false.

Large photos preserve their full frame. Thumbnails may be cropped by the interface. Individual image licenses apply independently of the Apache-licensed application code.
